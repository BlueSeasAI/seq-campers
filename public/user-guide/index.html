2. Caravans - the most-edited section

Add a new used caravan

  1. In the sidebar click 🏷️ Caravans → For Sale
  2. Top-right, click the + Create new button
  3. Fill in:
    • Caravan name - e.g. "2025 Kimberley Kruiser S Class S3"
    • URL slug - click the blue Generate button to auto-fill from the name
    • Brand - pick from the dropdown (Kimberley Kampers or Stockman Products)
    • Price - whole dollars only (no comma)
    • Status - For Sale
    • New or Used - USED
    • SEQ stock number - free text, can be letters + numbers (e.g. S3 or 1234)
    • Photos - see the next section for bulk upload
    • Description - use the toolbar for headings + bullets
    • Specifications - length, water capacity, ATM, Tare, etc.
    • Top 5 features - the "Is this right for you?" bullets shown on the listing
  4. Click Publish bottom-right
  5. The caravan is live at seqcampers.com.au/stock/your-url-slug within ~2 minutes

Upload many photos at once

Important: The Add item button only adds ONE photo at a time. To upload many, use drag-and-drop - it's the only way.
  1. Open File Explorer (Windows) or Finder (Mac) in a separate window
  2. Navigate to your caravan photo folder
  3. Select multiple photos:
    • Windows: Ctrl + click each photo, or Shift + click for a range, or Ctrl + A to select all
    • Mac: Cmd + click each photo, or Shift + click for a range, or Cmd + A to select all
  4. With them all highlighted, drag the selection into the dashed drop zone in the Photos field
  5. Sanity uploads them all in parallel
  6. Click each thumbnail after upload to add an Alt text (good for SEO)
  7. Drag thumbnails to reorder - the first photo is the main image on the listing

Mark a caravan as Sold

  1. Open the caravan
  2. Scroll to the Status field
  3. Change from For Sale to Sold
  4. Click Publish
  5. The listing automatically moves from /stock/ to the /sold/ archive within ~2 minutes

Delete a caravan

  1. Open the caravan you want to delete
  2. Click the actions menu (the three dots ⋮ near the Publish button, top-right)
  3. Click the prominent red 🗑 Delete this listing
  4. Confirm
  5. The caravan disappears from the website within ~2 minutes
Tip: Prefer marking as Sold instead of deleting. Sold caravans move to the /sold/ archive and act as social proof for future buyers.

3. Home page

Click 🏠 Home page in the sidebar. You'll see three editable sections.

Hero video (the looping video that plays full-screen at the top)

  1. Find the Hero video section
  2. Paste the YouTube URL into YouTube URL (e.g. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XYZ)
  3. Click Publish
  4. Live within ~2 minutes

Leave the URL blank to fall back to the default video.

Shane's Pick of the week

  1. Find the Shane's Pick section
  2. Caravan to feature - click and type to pick from your existing caravans. This caravan must already exist as a published listing.
  3. Original price (optional) - if you want a strike-through "was $X, now $Y" effect
  4. Shane's quote - a few sentences from Shane on why this one is special
  5. Status pill - pick Coming soon (orange) or Available now (green), or Hidden if no pill should show
  6. Click Publish

Three pathway videos (Watch / Visit / Adventure)

The three boxes under "Three steps to your next off-road adventure". Each has its own video.

  1. Find 1. Watch, 2. Visit, or 3. Adventure
  2. Paste the YouTube URL in each
  3. Optionally write a one-line sub-text shown under the label
  4. Click Publish

7. Service & workshop page

Click 🔧 Service & workshop page. Two sections.

Meet the crew (3 video tiles)

  1. For each of the 3 tiles (slot 1, 2, 3), paste a YouTube URL
  2. Add a short Caption shown under the tile (e.g. "Meet Grant in the workshop")
  3. Click Publish

Tiles 4-6 exist in the schema as unused carry-overs - you can ignore them.

This week in the workshop

One video, refreshed weekly, showing what's happening in the workshop right now.

  1. Find the This week in the workshop section
  2. Paste a fresh YouTube URL
  3. Add a Caption (e.g. "Mike fitting a lithium upgrade on a 2023 Karavan")
  4. Click Publish
  5. Repeat weekly - just overwrite the URL with the new clip

8. Shows page

Click 🎪 Shows page. Two sub-sections.

Add a new show (caravan show or event)

  1. Click Shows (caravan shows + events)
  2. Top-right click + Create new
  3. Fill in:
    • Title - e.g. "Brisbane Caravan Show 2027"
    • Slug - auto-generated, just click Generate
    • Status - Upcoming, Active (during the show), or Archived
    • Start date / End date
    • Venue name + Venue address
    • Stand number
    • Any show-special offers / on-stand caravans / etc.
  4. Click Publish

Shows page settings (intro + compilation video)

  1. Inside Shows page, click Shows page settings (intro + compilation video)
  2. Intro paragraph (optional) - shown above the list of upcoming shows
  3. Compilation video - the YouTube video shown at the top. Use it for a "highlights from past shows" reel
  4. Click Publish

9. Blog posts

Click 📝 Blog posts in the sidebar.

Write a new post

  1. Top-right click + Create new
  2. Fill in:
    • Title - the post headline. Keep under ~70 characters.
    • URL slug - click Generate
    • Published date - pick a date
    • Published? - tick when you're ready to go live. Untick to keep as a draft.
    • Cover image - upload one photo. Shown on the index tile + top of the post.
    • Excerpt - 1-2 sentences shown on the index page
    • Body - the full post text. Use the toolbar for headings, bullets, links, photos.
    • Author - defaults to "Shane & Maud"
  3. Click Publish
Tip: The placeholder lorem-ipsum post disappears automatically the moment you publish your first real post.

10. FAQs

Click ❓ FAQs. There are 16 starter FAQs already in place, written specifically for AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) so that when someone asks "best off-road caravan dealer Sunshine Coast", SEQ Campers gets cited.

Add a new FAQ

  1. Top-right click + Create new
  2. Question - phrase it exactly how a customer would ask it
  3. Answer - 1-3 sentences. First sentence should answer the question directly.
  4. Category - pick from the dropdown
  5. Display order - lower numbers appear first (use 10, 20, 30 so you can insert between)
  6. Published? - tick to make it live
  7. Click Publish

Edit an existing FAQ

  1. Click the FAQ in the list
  2. Edit the answer / wording
  3. Click Publish

11. AEO Keywords (search strategy)

Click 🎯 AEO Keywords. This is a reference table, not a live wiring - changing a keyword here doesn't automatically rewrite the website. It exists so you can see, sort and prioritise the search-strategy alongside the other content.

52 high-priority keywords are pre-loaded (30 Core + 22 Branded). They live under 5 sub-views:

  • 🔥 High priority - only the keywords flagged as priority 3
  • All keywords - the full sortable table
  • Not yet live on site - the ones still to be woven into the page copy
  • Branded only - brand + model name keywords
  • Core only - the primary intent keywords

When a keyword is added or marked high-priority here, the development team folds it into the relevant page copy on the next content pass. As keywords go live, click the Already live on site? tick on that keyword.

12. Handover pages (UNLISTED)

Click 🎁 Handover pages (UNLISTED). These pages are for new owners after they've bought a van. They show walk-through videos to watch before pickup.

UNLISTED means: these pages are NOT in the website menu. They're only accessible by direct URL - you share them via your printed handover letter or follow-up email. They don't appear in search engines.

Two pages exist:

  • Kimberley Kampers - at seqcampers.com.au/handover/kimberley. Sections for Karavan + Kruiswagen.
  • Stockman Products - at seqcampers.com.au/handover/stockman. Section for Rover 02.

Add or update videos

  1. Open a handover page (e.g. Kimberley Kampers)
  2. Scroll to Model sections
  3. Click a section to expand (e.g. Kimberley Karavan)
  4. Inside, click Videos → Add item to add a new video
  5. Fill in:
    • Video title - e.g. "Karavan setup walkthrough"
    • YouTube URL
    • Short description (optional)
  6. Drag to reorder if needed
  7. Click Publish

Add a new model section

  1. Open the handover page
  2. Scroll to Model sections
  3. Click Add item
  4. Fill in Model name + Section intro heading
  5. Add videos as above
  6. Click Publish

13. Site-wide settings

Click ⚙️ Site-wide settings. Two sections.

Show Special banner

The thin coloured strip that runs across the very top of every page (e.g. "Get it before the Brisbane Caravan Show ends Sun 8 June").

  1. Banner headline - short, urgency-led. Leave blank to hide the banner entirely.
  2. Banner end date (optional) - when this date passes the banner auto-hides, so you don't have to remember to switch it off
  3. CTA link text + CTA link URL - an optional clickable link after the headline
  4. Click Publish

Reserve $1,000 CTA

The "Reserve this van for $1,000" Stripe button shown on the New Caravans page.

  1. Show the Reserve button? - tick to enable
  2. Stripe payment link URL - paste the Stripe link from your Stripe dashboard
  3. Click Publish

14. What happens when you save?

You click Publish in Sanity → Netlify rebuilds the website → Live at seqcampers.com.au

Total time: ~2 minutes

Refresh your browser at seqcampers.com.au to see the change. If you don't see it after 5 minutes, try a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows, Cmd+Shift+R on Mac) - sometimes your browser shows a cached version.

Good to know: Edits don't go live until you click Publish. You can save drafts and come back later - nothing on the live website changes until you publish.

Every form on the website sends an email to sales@seqcampers.com.au. There are 7 forms:
